# S-10: Hàn Market, Cock Church and Cham Museum landmarks

- **Priority:** P0 (milestone M1 Map)
- **Covers:** FR-05 · **Decisions:** A-05, NFR-03, NFR-04
- **Depends on stories:** S-06

## User story
As a player, I want Hàn Market, the Cock Church (Nhà thờ Con Gà) and the Cham Museum on the map, so that I can tell I am in Da Nang.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Hàn Market**
   - Given the map
   - When I look at the market area
   - Then a market building with a readable sign is there
2. **Cock Church**
   - Given the map
   - When I look at the church area
   - Then a church with its recognizable shape and tower is there
3. **Cham Museum**
   - Given the map
   - When I look at the museum area
   - Then a museum building with a recognizable Cham style is there
4. **Not overlapping**
   - Given the landmarks
   - When I look at the layout
   - Then none overlaps a street, the river or a house

## Out of scope
- Dragon Bridge (S-09); decorations (deferred P1: FR-07).

## Notes
- No real brand names on signs and no government-agency buildings (NFR-03, NFR-04).
- Any text on signs is Vietnamese (NFR-15).
