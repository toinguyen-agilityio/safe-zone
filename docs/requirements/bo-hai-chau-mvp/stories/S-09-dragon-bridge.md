# S-09: Dragon Bridge looks like a dragon

- **Priority:** P0 (milestone M1 Map)
- **Covers:** FR-05 · **Decisions:** A-05 (all four landmarks required), NFR-03
- **Depends on stories:** S-08

## User story
As a player, I want the Dragon Bridge to have a dragon body, head, horns and tail, so that I recognize it from far away.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Dragon body**
   - Given the Dragon Bridge from S-08
   - When I look at it
   - Then a tube-shaped dragon body runs along the bridge
2. **Head, horns, tail**
   - Given the dragon
   - When I look at both ends
   - Then one end has a head with horns and the other a tail
3. **Recognizable from far**
   - Given I stand far from the bridge
   - When I look at it
   - Then it is recognizable as the Dragon Bridge

## Out of scope
- Fire breathing event (deferred P1: FR-38, FR-39).
