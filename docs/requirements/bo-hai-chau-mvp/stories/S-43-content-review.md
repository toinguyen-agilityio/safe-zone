# S-43: Content review for legal and sensitivity rules

- **Priority:** P0 (milestone M7 Release MVP)
- **Covers:** NFR-03, NFR-04 · **Decisions:** R-04
- **Depends on stories:** S-40

## User story
As the stakeholder, I want the content checked against the rules, so that the game is safe to share with friends.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **No copyrighted assets**
   - Given all models, textures, fonts and sounds
   - When I review them
   - Then none is copyrighted or taken from PUBG
2. **No real brands**
   - Given signs and names on the map
   - When I review them
   - Then no real brand name appears
3. **No government buildings**
   - Given the map
   - When I review it
   - Then no government-agency headquarters is modelled
4. **Cartoon violence**
   - Given the effects and end-screen lines
   - When I review them
   - Then there is no blood or gore
5. **Review recorded**
   - Given the review
   - When it is finished
   - Then findings and fixes are listed in the story

## Out of scope
- Game license check (after MVP).
